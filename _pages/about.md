<div class="project-content" markdown="1">
  <p>
    I am a Human-AI Interaction researcher with a background in Computer Science and experience across academia and enterprise software. My research examines how people understand and adapt to intelligent systems, and how these systems shape behaviour, learning and decision-making.
  </p>
  <p>
    During my PhD in Human-Computer Interaction at Sorbonne Université, I studied adaptive AI systems for motor learning. My work examined both what these systems enable people to achieve and how people form mental models of their capabilities and predictions. More recently, I have also studied how professional artists integrate and make sense of generative AI in their creative practice.
  </p>
  <p>
    Across these projects, I have become particularly interested in the relationship between system behaviour and human understanding: how people interpret what a technology does, how that understanding develops through interaction, and how it influences the way they use and respond to it.
  </p>
  <p>
    I am interested in applying this perspective to real-world problems where understanding both people and technology matters. I particularly enjoy research that combines qualitative and quantitative evidence to uncover how people interact with systems and translate those findings into practical improvements. My earlier experience implementing enterprise software for AXA and L’Oréal also gives me a perspective on how technology, user needs and organisational contexts come together in practice.
  </p>

  <p><strong>What I'm interested in</strong></p> 
  <ul>
    <li>How people form and update mental models of intelligent and adaptive systems</li>
    <li>How increasing system autonomy changes what people need to understand to interact effectively with AI</li>
    <li>How people interpret system capabilities, outputs and behaviour when deciding whether to rely on or question them</li>
    <li>Designing empirical studies that combine qualitative and quantitative methods to understand Human-AI interaction</li>
    <li>Translating research findings into practical principles for the design of intelligent systems</li> 
  </ul>

  <h2>Education &amp; Previous Experience</h2>
  <p>
    I hold a PhD in Human-Computer Interaction from Sorbonne Université and a Master's degree in Computer Science from Université Grenoble Alpes, following a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Mauritius.
  </p>
  <p>
    Before my PhD, my research experience included work on information search and visual attention using eye-tracking, as well as computational approaches to video summarisation.
    Before moving into research, I worked as a Salesforce Consultant, contributing to enterprise software projects for AXA and L'Oréal. This experience gave me an early perspective on translating business and user requirements into technical solutions.
  </p>
